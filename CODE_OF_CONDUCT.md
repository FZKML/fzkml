# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Critique evidence and ideas, avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and retaliation. These
expectations apply in project issues, reviews, discussions, and project events.

Report concerns privately to **Guillaume Lauzier at guillaumelauzier@gmail.com**,
the monitored enforcement route supplied by the founding maintainer. Include only
information needed for handling the concern. Do not publish private reports in
issues. For a conflict involving this maintainer, use
[GitHub's abuse reporting](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) where
applicable; no independent project enforcement officer is currently appointed.

The maintainer acknowledges when available, gathers relevant information, limits
access to those handling the report, considers conflicts, and communicates next
steps to the reporter. Responses may include a warning, removal of content,
temporary restriction, or exclusion proportionate to conduct. Reconsideration can
be requested privately with new evidence. There is no guaranteed response time;
do not use this channel for emergencies. Do not retaliate against good-faith reports.
